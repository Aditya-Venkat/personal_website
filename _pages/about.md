## 🔬 Research Interests
My research interests broadly span:
- **Bayesian inference and hierarchical modeling** — developing flexible, interpretable probabilistic models that incorporate prior knowledge.  
- **Computational genomics** — leveraging statistical frameworks to analyze transcriptomic, spatial, and single-cell data.  
- **Survival and reliability analysis** — modeling heterogeneous survival data and competing risks using Bayesian and semi-parametric methods.  
- **Machine learning for biology** — using generative models, probabilistic embeddings, LLMs and causal inference in biomedical data.
